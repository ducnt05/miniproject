7. CHAY OBJECT DETECTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Mo file:

    D:\SPCV\miniproject\detection\EELAN_Detection.ipynb

Chay cac cell theo thu tu:

1. Cau hinh PROJECT_ROOT, DATA_ROOT, OUTPUT_ROOT va import.
2. Load va kiem tra train/valid/test dataset.
3. Kiem tra forward pass va loss.
4. Chay overfit tren 10 anh.
5. Chay full training.
6. Dinh nghia decoder, IoU, NMS va evaluator.
7. Load checkpoint tot nhat va tinh metrics.
8. Chay threshold sweep.
9. Ve loss curve va bounding box inference.

Cac module duoc notebook import:

    detection\dataset.py
        YOLODetectionDataset va detection_collate.

    detection\model.py
        ConvBNAct, channel_shuffle, EELANLite,
        EELANBackbone, FPNNeck va EELANDetector.

    detection\train_detection.py
        build_targets, detection_loss va training loop tham khao.

Kien truc detection:

    E-ELAN Backbone -> FPN Neck -> Single-scale Detection Head

Output head co dang:

    B x (5 + so_class) x S x S
    = B x 8 x 32 x 32

Trong 8 kenh:

    4 kenh bounding box
    1 kenh objectness
    3 kenh class score

Weights detection:

    D:\SPCV\miniproject\detection\outputs\best_detection.pt

Lich su train:

    D:\SPCV\miniproject\detection\outputs\history.json

9. CACH SAVE/LOAD WEIGHTS VA INFERENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Classification:

    Weights:
    classification\outputs\cls_eelan_improved\best.pt

    Mo notebook classification, nap checkpoint va chay cell inference.

Detection:

    Weights:
    detection\outputs\best_detection.pt

    Mo notebook detection, chay cell load checkpoint, sau do chay cell
    decode_predictions va show_prediction.

Inference detection thuc hien:

1. Forward anh qua model.
2. Decode output thanh bounding box xyxy.
3. Tinh confidence = objectness * class probability.
4. Loc theo confidence threshold.
5. Ap dung NMS theo tung class.
6. Ve box, ten class va confidence len anh.


10. CAC HAN CHE CAN GHI TRONG BAO CAO
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Detector chi du doan single-scale.
- Moi grid cell chi luu mot object, object trung cung cell co the bi bo qua.
- Detection Head va loss la ban toi gian, khong phai YOLOv7 day du.
- Chua su dung nhieu scale, anchor/anchor-free head day du hoac CIoU loss.
- Ket qua panda detection thap hon cat va dog.
- Co hien tuong overfitting: train loss giam nhung validation loss tang.
- mAP@50 tu cai dat trong notebook khong nen goi la COCO mAP chuan.

Day la cac han che cua kien truc thu nghiem, khong phai loi khi chay
notebook.
